Bradley Atnip - Investigation of the Battery
It was a privilege to investigate one of the most iconic parts of New York City. My professors assigned my team to The Battery, which encompasses FiDi, TriBeCa, and Battery Park City. This series of projects highlights the layers of investigation we conducted to develop an in-depth understanding of the site.

01 / Initial Investigation of The Battery

DTECH

Interactive QGIS Spatial Map

By learning QGIS in DTECH, The Battery was investigated through the lens of understanding land value and flood risk. The following assignment highlights my process of developing a general understanding of the site’s climate risks.

02 / Constructing the Past

Urban Design Studio

Layered Site History Scroll

For this project, my teammates and I investigated the history of The Battery through a series of layers. We rendered a nine-foot scroll that dives into many of the minute elements within the site’s history and context. Our argument demonstrates how the site’s opportunities have also led to its vulnerabilities, revealing how its conditions can shift from positive to negative.

03 / Zine of the Present

Urban Design Studio

Workers of Lower Manhattan Zine

The zine highlights the everyday workers of Lower Manhattan. We conducted a series of interviews and site observations to craft a document that brings attention to this often unseen group within The Battery.

04 / Plural Futures

Urban Design Studio

Waterfront Wager Board Game

For Plural Futures, our board game, Waterfront Wager, highlights agency decision-making and negotiation along a flooding waterfront. As the game progresses and flooding occurs, players must work together to retain the properties they have acquired.

Overall, these four exercises enabled me to develop a deeper understanding of the multiple layers of social and environmental complexity that define The Battery.
